GradatiON is a personal Android client for language models: chat, roleplay with your own characters, and a remote for the coding agents on your computers. Everything sits under one quiet, monochrome Liquid Glass interface.

• Chat: streaming markdown replies, reasoning, vision, image generation, web search, presets and optional tool calling
• Roleplay: your own characters, personas and lorebooks; a Memory the character keeps true; Continue to let the story move on without you
• Code: pair your computers and drive Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and more; approve edits, read colored diffs, get notified when an agent needs you
• Voice input in every composer: tap, talk, tap. Words paste into the field and never send by themselves
• Built-in demo model: try the app with no key or network
• OpenRouter in the cloud, or Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, MLX LM, oMLX, Nativ and Hermes Agent on your LAN
• History with autosave, search, pin, rename, import and export
• Encrypted chat database; no trackers, analytics or ads

Fork of oxproxion (Apache 2.0). Not affiliated with xAI, Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter.
